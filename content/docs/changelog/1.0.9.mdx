### Systems

<ChangelogList>
    <Added>Functional volume knobs on audio control panels</Added>
    <Added>Update available dialog on iniManager releases</Added>

    <Fixed>Aircraft stuck with black screens in MSFS 2020 unless WASM cache was cleared</Fixed>
    <Fixed>Rework the communication bus between systems and avionics to reduce throughput and improve performance [^1]</Fixed>
    <Fixed>FMS `FPLN FUEL` values cannot be entered when IRSs are not aligned</Fixed>
    <Fixed>FMS `PERF DEP` `ACCEL` and `THR RED` altitudes not updating when corresponding heights are set</Fixed>
    <Fixed>Lowered gains on `APPR LOC1/2` tracking to prevent over-aggressive turns on final</Fixed>
    <Fixed>FMS ocasionally freezes on `PENDING` state when uplinking flight plan with native navdata</Fixed>
    <Fixed>Holding `PILOT EVENT` button should dismiss `FDR ACCEL FAIL` message</Fixed>
    <Fixed>Standby instrument barometer knob does not set altimeter</Fixed>
    <Fixed>Approach feather on VSD should not move with the aircraft</Fixed>
    <Fixed>Aircraft icon should clamp to screen edge `MAP PLAN` format</Fixed>
    <Fixed>Oil temperature and pressure on EICAS should match color on `SYN STATUS` format</Fixed>
    <Fixed>`SELECT FIX` dialog for runways shows incorrect name in header</Fixed>
    <Fixed>Rollout bar moves up/down instead of left/right</Fixed>
    <Fixed>Set `KOHLSMAN SETTING MB`, `KOHLSMAN SETTING HG`, and `KOHLSMAN SETTING STD` variables [^2]</Fixed>
    <Fixed>Livery-specific configurations are not always applied correctly</Fixed>
    <Fixed>Takeoff and landing weights in `FMS PERF` should be rounded</Fixed>
    <Fixed>VNAV not transitioning to `VFLC` automatically when crossing the acceleration altitude</Fixed>
    <Fixed>VNAV not transitioning to `VPATH` automatically at top of descent</Fixed>
    <Fixed>LPV approaches lose `VGP` guidance after passing final approach fix</Fixed>
    <Fixed>Altitude trend vector should not be shown on an invalid VSD</Fixed>
    <Fixed>Aircraft does not always initialize correctly on in-flight save states</Fixed>
</ChangelogList>
